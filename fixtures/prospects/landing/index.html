Frequently asked questions

How much history do I need?

Twelve months of sales history gives the best results, but forecasts start working with as little as twelve weeks.

Can I override a forecast?

Yes. Buyers can adjust any forecast, and Larkspur records the override so you can see later whether it helped.

Is my sales data shared with anyone?

No. Your data is used only to train your own forecasts and is never pooled with other customers or sold.
